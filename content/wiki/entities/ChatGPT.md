# ChatGPT

Consumer-facing AI chat interface developed by [[OpenAI]], launched November 2022. Built on GPT-3.5 initially, later GPT-4. Triggered the current mainstream wave of AI adoption across education, healthcare, and professional domains.

## Relevance to this wiki
- Cited as the AI tool students most commonly use in assessment contexts ([[Assessment Design for a Time of AI — Dawson 2025]])
- Exemplifies the challenge of "AI can do my assessment" that is driving assessment redesign
- A primary context for [[Evaluative Judgment]] development — students need to judge ChatGPT outputs
