# Jens Rasmussen

Danish safety engineer and cognitive systems researcher. Pioneer of cognitive systems engineering and human error analysis in complex sociotechnical systems.

## Key contribution to this wiki

[[Skills Rules and Knowledge — Rasmussen 1983]] — the SRK (Skills-Rules-Knowledge) framework, which describes three levels of cognitive performance:
- **Skill-based**: automated sensorimotor responses; errors = slips and lapses
- **Rule-based**: stored if-then rules for familiar situations; errors = misapplication of good rules
- **Knowledge-based**: deliberate reasoning for novel situations; errors = limited working memory, confirmation bias

## Significance

SRK provides the cognitive taxonomy underlying all discussion of how AI changes clinician cognition. AI systems can absorb skill-based tasks; rule-based tasks are the primary current target; knowledge-based tasks require genuine reasoning and are hardest to automate.
