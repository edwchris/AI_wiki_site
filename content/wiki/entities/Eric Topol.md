Cardiologist, genomics researcher, and digital medicine authority; Executive Vice-President, Professor, and Founder of the Scripps Research Translational Institute, La Jolla, California.

## Research Focus
- AI and deep learning applications in clinical medicine
- Digital health: wearables, remote monitoring, genomics
- Clinical AI evaluation, policy, and ethics

## Key Publications (in this wiki)
- [[Role Separation in AI-Human Radiology — Rajpurkar & Topol 2025]] — co-author arguing for role separation over assistive AI paradigm
- [[AI vs. Clinicians — Nagendran et al. 2020]] — co-author of systematic review revealing methodological weaknesses in AI vs. clinician studies

## Books
- *Deep Medicine: How Artificial Intelligence Can Make Healthcare Human Again* (2019) — argues AI can handle repetitive tasks and restore the patient-physician relationship; widely read in digital health community
