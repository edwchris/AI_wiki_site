# Tesla

Electric vehicle and energy company known for its AI-driven Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) systems. Relevant to this wiki primarily through [[Andrej Karpathy]], who led Tesla's Autopilot AI team from 2017–2022 and built one of the most ambitious real-world AI deployment programs in history.
