# Jessie Childs

Sonography researcher and educator based in Australia. Co-author on the two foundational papers establishing the current national professional standards for Australian sonographers.

## Key Publications (in this wiki)

- [[Australian Sonographer Competency Framework — Edwards et al. 2022]] — Delphi study (n=55 expert panel, 6 rounds) developing the four-component national competency framework for Australian sonographers via ASAR
- [[Entrustable Professional Activities in Sonography — Edwards et al. 2023]] — industry survey (n=309) applying EPA framework to define what examinations can be entrusted to graduate sonographers
