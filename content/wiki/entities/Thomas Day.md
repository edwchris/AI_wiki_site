Clinical researcher; lead author of the PROMETHEUS randomized controlled trial (2025) — the first prospective RCT of AI assistance in routine fetal anomaly ultrasound screening.

## Key Publication (in this wiki)
- [[PROMETHEUS RCT — Day et al. 2025]] — RCT demonstrating 42% reduction in fetal anomaly scan time with AI assistance, maintained diagnostic performance, and reduced sonographer cognitive load

## Significance
The PROMETHEUS trial represents a methodological advance in clinical AI evaluation, addressing the prospective RCT gap identified in [[AI vs. Clinicians — Nagendran et al. 2020]].
