# Thomas Malone

Professor at MIT Sloan School of Management; founding director of the [[MIT Center for Collective Intelligence]].

## Work
- Research on collective intelligence, organizational design, and human–AI collaboration
- Co-author of [[When Combinations of Humans and AI Are Useful — Vaccaro et al. 2024]]
- Corresponding author for the 2024 meta-analysis (malone@mit.edu)

## Funding (as of 2024)
- Toyota Research Institute
- MIT Quest for Intelligence
- National Research Foundation Singapore (CREATE programme)
