# Ziad Obermeyer

Associate Professor at UC Berkeley School of Public Health. Physician and health economist working at the intersection of machine learning, health policy, and health equity. Known for empirical work exposing racial bias in commercial healthcare algorithms.

## Key contribution to this wiki

**[[Racial Bias in Health Algorithm — Obermeyer et al. 2019]]** — dissected racial bias in a commercial population health management algorithm used by ~200 million patients per year. Showed that training on healthcare cost (not health status) as a proxy embeds structural racial inequality. Key finding: Black patients are 26.3% sicker than White patients at the same algorithmic risk score.

The paper's central contribution to the field is demonstrating that bias can arise from reasonable-seeming label choices, not malicious design — and that it is fixable by changing the training label.
