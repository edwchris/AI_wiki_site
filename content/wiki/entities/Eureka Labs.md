# Eureka Labs

AI-native education company founded by [[Andrej Karpathy]] in 2024. Mission: build AI-augmented learning environments where AI teaching assistants, alongside human instructors, can deliver world-class education at scale. The first product is an AI-assisted version of Karpathy's neural networks / deep learning course.
