UX researcher and human factors specialist; conducted fieldwork for Google Health's clinical AI deployment studies; known for [[Human-Centered AI Evaluation]] methodology applied to real-world clinical AI systems.

## Key Publication (in this wiki)
- [[Human-Centered Evaluation of AI in Diabetic Retinopathy Clinics — Beede et al. 2020]] — ethnographic evaluation of Google Health's diabetic retinopathy AI across 11 clinics in Thailand; documented real-world failure modes invisible to benchmark evaluation

## Significance
The Beede et al. 2020 study is one of the most cited examples of the gap between AI benchmark performance and clinical deployment reality. It demonstrates that socio-environmental factors — not just model limitations — determine whether an AI system succeeds in practice.
