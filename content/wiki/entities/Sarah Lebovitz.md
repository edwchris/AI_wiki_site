# Sarah Lebovitz

IS researcher at the University of Virginia McIntire School of Commerce. Studies the management and organizational implications of AI in knowledge work, particularly healthcare settings.

- **Affiliation**: UVA McIntire School of Commerce
- **Research focus**: AI in organizations, knowledge work, healthcare AI, evaluation of ML systems

## Key Work

- [[Is AI Ground Truth Really True — Lebovitz, Levina & Lifshitz-Assaf 2021]] — field study on why high-accuracy AI tools fail in clinical practice; introduces the know-what/know-how distinction applied to [[AI Ground Truth]]
