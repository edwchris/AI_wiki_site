# OpenAI

AI research organisation and company founded 2015; developers of the GPT series, DALL-E, Codex, and ChatGPT. Originally a nonprofit, restructured to a capped-profit model. Among the most influential organisations in the development and commercialisation of large language models.

## Key products (relevant to this wiki)
- **GPT-4 / GPT-4o**: frontier language models
- **[[ChatGPT]]**: consumer-facing chat interface; triggered mainstream AI adoption wave from Nov 2022
- **o1 / o3**: reasoning-focused models with chain-of-thought capabilities

## Connections
- [[Andrej Karpathy]] — co-founder; led Tesla Autopilot; returned to OpenAI then founded Eureka Labs
- [[ChatGPT]] — the product most visible to end users
