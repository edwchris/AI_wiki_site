# Mica Endsley

American cognitive engineer. Developed the dominant formal model of situation awareness (SA) used across aviation, military, medicine, and now AI-human interaction research.

## Key contribution to this wiki

[[Toward a Theory of Situation Awareness — Endsley 1995]] — formalises SA as a three-level model:
1. **Level 1 — Perception**: detecting relevant cues in the environment
2. **Level 2 — Comprehension**: integrating cues into a current situation model
3. **Level 3 — Projection**: predicting future states based on current understanding

Automation primarily supports Level 1 (sensing), but can actively degrade Levels 2 and 3 by removing the active processing that builds comprehension and projection.

## The "out-of-the-loop" problem

When automation handles Level 1 for the operator, the operator's Level 2 and 3 SA degrades — they are "out of the loop." When automation fails, they cannot quickly rebuild SA to take over effectively. This is the formal mechanism behind Bainbridge's third irony.
