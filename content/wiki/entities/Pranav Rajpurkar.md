Assistant Professor of Biomedical Informatics at Harvard Medical School; leading researcher in AI evaluation for medical imaging and human-AI collaboration in clinical settings.

## Research Focus
- Rigorous evaluation of AI performance in radiology and clinical tasks
- Human-AI collaboration models; when and how AI improves clinical outcomes
- Foundation models for medical imaging

## Key Publications (in this wiki)
- [[Role Separation in AI-Human Radiology — Rajpurkar & Topol 2025]] — argues that the current assistive AI paradigm is failing and proposes role separation as an alternative
- [[AI in Health and Medicine — Rajpurkar et al. 2022]] — comprehensive review of AI applications and limitations across clinical medicine

## Notable Earlier Work
- CheXNet (Stanford, 2017): deep learning model for chest X-ray interpretation claiming radiologist-level pneumonia detection — one of the landmark papers assessed for methodological rigor in [[AI vs. Clinicians — Nagendran et al. 2020]]
