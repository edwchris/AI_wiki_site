# Trisha Greenhalgh

Professor of Primary Care Health Sciences at the University of Oxford. A leading figure in qualitative and mixed-methods health research, implementation science, and the critique of technocentric approaches to healthcare. Known for evidence-based medicine, complexity theory in healthcare, and technology implementation frameworks.

## Key contribution to this wiki

**[[Beyond Adoption — NASSS Framework — Greenhalgh et al. 2017]]** — the NASSS framework for understanding why health technologies fail to be adopted, scaled, or sustained. Based on a hermeneutic systematic review of 28 frameworks plus 6 longitudinal empirical case studies across 20+ UK healthcare organisations.

## Other notable work

- Systematic reviews of evidence-based medicine
- Research on remote consulting and video consultations
- Critique of "magic bullet" thinking in digital health
- Prominent voice on complexity theory in healthcare
