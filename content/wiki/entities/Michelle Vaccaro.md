# Michelle Vaccaro

Researcher at [[MIT Center for Collective Intelligence]] (Sloan School of Management) and the Institute for Data, Systems, and Society (Schwarzman College of Computing), MIT.

## Work
- Lead author of the landmark 2024 meta-analysis on human–AI synergy: [[When Combinations of Humans and AI Are Useful — Vaccaro et al. 2024]]
- Research focus: human–AI collaboration, collective intelligence, experimental methods
