# Lisanne Bainbridge

British ergonomist and cognitive scientist. Author of one of the most cited papers in human factors literature.

## Key contribution to this wiki

[[Ironies of Automation — Bainbridge 1983]] — argues that automation creates harder residual tasks for human operators, erodes the skills needed to handle those tasks, and fails precisely when humans are least capable of taking over. Published in *Automatica*, 1983.

The three ironies:
1. The more automated a system, the harder the residual manual task
2. Automation erodes the skills needed to perform that residual task
3. Automation fails unpredictably, requiring human intervention at the worst moments
