Associate Professor in Medical Ultrasound at Queensland University of Technology (QUT), Brisbane, Australia. Co-lead of QUT's Quantitative Ultrasound Imaging (QUIQ) research group. Former Chair and Board Director of the Australasian Sonographer Accreditation Registry (ASAR).

## Research Focus
- AI applications in diagnostic ultrasound and professional implications for sonography
- Sonographer training pathways, competency frameworks, and work-integrated learning
- Placental elastography and obstetric imaging
- [[Deliberate Practice]] in clinical skill development

## Key Publications (in this wiki)
- [[Deliberate Practice in Sonography — Edwards et al. 2018]] — foundational paper on deliberate practice theory applied to sonography education
- [[AI in the Sonography Profession — Edwards et al. 2022]] — overview of AI applications and their professional and educational implications for sonographers
- [[The Role of Patient Outcomes in Moral Responsibility — Edwards et al. 2025]] — empirical study of how outcome framing shifts moral responsibility attribution in AI-assisted decisions
- [[Measuring AI Preparedness in Health Professions Education — Edwards et al. 2026]] — national survey benchmarking AI preparedness among Australian MRS students and graduates
- [[Learning Curve for Left Atrial Strain Analysis — Edwards et al. 2024]] — brief report on minimum cases for LA strain analysis competency (co-authored with Queensland echo group)
- [[Australian Sonographer Competency Framework — Edwards et al. 2022]] — Delphi study developing the national competency framework for Australian sonographers
- [[Entrustable Professional Activities in Sonography — Edwards et al. 2023]] — industry survey applying EPA framework to sonography; finding that emergency/on-call work excluded at graduate level
