# Phillip Dawson

Professor at **Deakin University** (Melbourne, Australia). Co-director of the **Centre for Research in Assessment and Digital Learning (CRADLE)**. Leading international researcher in feedback literacy, assessment design, and academic integrity — increasingly focused on generative AI and assessment.

---

## Research Focus

- **Feedback literacy** — student and teacher competencies for engaging productively with feedback
- **Assessment design** — particularly in higher education and digital learning contexts
- **Academic integrity** — cheating, contract cheating, and the ethics of AI use in assessment
- **AI and assessment** — redesigning assessment for a world where AI can complete most unsupervised tasks

---

## Key Contributions

| Contribution | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Feedback literacy (with Boud & Carless)** | Model of student feedback literacy: appreciating, managing affect, judging, acting. Teacher feedback literacy: 19 competencies across macro/meso/micro levels |
| **"What feedback literate teachers do"** | Study identifying meso-level design (not micro comments) as the key leverage point for improving feedback uptake |
| **TEXA guidance on AI and assessment** | Co-developed (with Lodge, Howard, Beerman) national Australian guidance on assessment in the AI age: 2 guiding principles + 5 propositions |
| **Structural vs. discursive change** | Framework distinguishing instruction-based (discursive) from design-based (structural) responses to AI in assessment; argues structural change is necessary |
| **Swiss cheese security model** | Multiple imperfect assessment security layers layered together; no single "secure" format required |
