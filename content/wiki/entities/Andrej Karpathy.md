# Andrej Karpathy

**Kind:** Person — AI researcher and educator

## Overview
Prominent AI researcher known for work on deep learning, computer vision, and language models. Formerly Director of AI at Tesla (Autopilot) and founding member/research scientist at OpenAI. Known for highly accessible educational content (neural networks from scratch, micrograd, nanoGPT series). Author of the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]].

## Key contributions
- **LLM Wiki Pattern** — methodology for building LLM-maintained personal knowledge bases (see [[LLM Wiki Pattern — Karpathy]])
- **[[nanoGPT]]** — minimal GPT implementation for education
- **[[micrograd]]** — tiny autograd engine for teaching backpropagation
- **Andrej Karpathy YouTube channel** — "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" series
- [[Tesla]] Autopilot AI direction
- Co-founder of [[OpenAI]]
