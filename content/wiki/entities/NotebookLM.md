# NotebookLM

A Google AI-powered research assistant that uses [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] to answer questions grounded in user-provided documents. Users upload sources (PDFs, docs, web pages); NotebookLM synthesises answers citing those sources only, reducing hallucination.

## Relevance
- Implements the RAG pattern for personal knowledge work
- Alternative to the [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] for source-grounded Q&A — but less persistent (answers not saved as wiki pages)

## Connections
- [[Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] — the architectural pattern underlying NotebookLM
- [[LLM Wiki Pattern]] — complementary approach; LLM Wiki builds persistent structured knowledge
