Radiologist and informatics researcher affiliated with the American College of Radiology (ACR); lead author of the landmark 2019 joint multisociety statement on ethics of AI in radiology.

## Key Publication (in this wiki)
- [[Ethics of AI in Radiology — Geis et al. 2019]] — joint statement from seven North American and European radiology societies (ACR, RSNA, ESR, SIIM, ESMI, CAR, AAPM) establishing ethical principles for AI in radiology; remains a foundational reference in clinical AI governance
