**Citation:** Karni, O., Shitrit, I. B., Perlin, A. et al. (2025). AI-enhanced guidance demonstrated improvement in novices' Apical-4-chamber and Apical-5-chamber views. *BMC Medical Education*, 25, 558. DOI: 10.1186/s12909-025-06905-5

**Raw file:** `s12909-025-06905-5.pdf`

## Summary
Prospective RCT evaluating a real-time AI-based guidance tool for cardiac ultrasound (echocardiography) training in novices. AI provides step-by-step probe navigation guidance and image quality feedback during training and proficiency testing. Demonstrates that AI-enhanced guidance significantly improves novice performance on apical cardiac views — one of the most challenging views in echocardiography.

## Study Design
- 44 participants; 21 AI-enhanced group, 23 control group
- 6-hour cardiac US training course for both groups
- AI-enhanced group: additional real-time AI feedback on probe navigation and image quality during training AND testing
- Primary outcome: cardiac US proficiency exam (apical 4-chamber and 5-chamber views)

## Key Findings
- AI-enhanced group significantly outperformed control on both apical views at proficiency exam
- AI guidance improved probe manipulation precision and image quality attainment
- No adverse effects on learning or confidence reported

## Critical Tension
This paper directly creates a tension with [[Ironies of Automation]] and [[Deliberate Practice]]:
- **Pro-AI case**: AI guidance accelerates novice acquisition of a technically difficult skill; lowers the barrier to POCUS competency
- **Anti-AI case**: Novices guided by AI may not develop the independent hand-eye coordination to acquire views without AI (dependency risk)
- The RCT demonstrates **short-term performance improvement** but does not follow up on **retention and transfer** without AI support — the key question

## Connection to Broader Themes
- Supports the [[Topol Review — Health Education England 2019]] vision of AI as a training accelerator
- Contrasts with [[Psychomotor Skills in Ultrasound — Nicholls et al. 2014]] concern that AI removes the deliberate practice loop
- A key open question: what happens when trained-with-AI novices try to scan without AI?
