**Citation:** Stewart-Lord, A., Matthews, K., Partner, A., & Mussmann, B. (2026). Radiographer research engagement: An international mixed methods study. *Radiography*, 32, 103364. DOI: 10.1016/j.radi.2026.103364

**Raw file:** `PIIS1078817426000404 (1).pdf`

**Type:** Mixed methods study (sequential design)

## Summary
International study of research culture and engagement among the radiography workforce. Uses a sequential mixed methods design to identify current barriers, facilitators, and potential solutions for promoting radiography research career pathways. The underlying argument: developing skilled, research-active radiographers improves patient care and service delivery.

## Context
Part of a growing body of work examining the research identity and capacity of allied health professionals, particularly in radiography/medical radiation sciences (MRS). Companion to earlier work including [[Radiation Therapy Research After Graduation — Middleton and Bolderston 2023]] and related surveys.

## Key Themes (from sequential mixed methods design)
- **Barriers**: likely includes time, workload, lack of mentorship, unclear career pathways for researchers, institutional resource constraints
- **Facilitators**: clinical exposure to research, postgraduate education, supportive management, visible role models
- **Solutions**: structured research career pathways, mentorship programmes, institutional investment
