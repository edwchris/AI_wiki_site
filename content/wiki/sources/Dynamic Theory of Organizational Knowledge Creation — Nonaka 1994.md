**Citation:** Nonaka, I. (1994). A dynamic theory of organizational knowledge creation. *Organization Science*, 5(1), 14–37.

**Raw file:** `Nonaka_1994_A dynamic thoery of organizational.pdf`

## Core Contribution
Introduces the **SECI model** — four modes of knowledge conversion between tacit and explicit knowledge — as the engine of organizational knowledge creation.

## The SECI Model

| Mode | Direction | Description | Example |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Socialization** | Tacit → Tacit | Sharing tacit knowledge through shared experience | Apprenticeship, on-the-job training, observation |
| **Externalization** | Tacit → Explicit | Converting tacit knowledge to explicit concepts | Articulating a protocol, writing a case study, creating a metaphor |
| **Combination** | Explicit → Explicit | Recombining explicit knowledge | Database integration, literature synthesis |
| **Internalization** | Explicit → Tacit | Absorbing explicit knowledge into tacit competence | Learning by doing, reading → competence |

## Knowledge Spiral
Knowledge creation proceeds as an upward spiral:
- Starts at the **individual** level
- Moves to the **group** level
- Then to the **organizational** level
- Can extend to the **inter-organizational** level
Each spiral traverses all four SECI modes

## Tacit vs. Explicit Knowledge
- **Tacit**: personal, context-specific, hard to formalize; embedded in skill, intuition, and judgment (Polanyi's "we know more than we can tell")
- **Explicit**: codifiable, transmissible in language, symbols, or numbers

## Relevance to AI
AI systems operate primarily in the **Combination** mode — pattern-matching over vast explicit data. Key limitations:

- **Training labels** = a lossy Externalization of expert tacit judgment; the surrounding know-how is stripped away → core of the [[AI Ground Truth]] problem
- **Socialization** (T→T): AI cannot share experiential knowledge the way humans do
- **Internalization** (E→T): AI does not learn tacit competence through embodied practice
- **Externalization gap**: AI produces outputs (predictions) but cannot reliably articulate the tacit reasoning behind them
