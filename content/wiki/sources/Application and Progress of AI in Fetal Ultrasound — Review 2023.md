## Summary
Narrative review of AI applications specifically in fetal ultrasound, covering standard plane detection, biometric parameter measurement, and anomaly detection. Provides an up-to-date (2023) picture of AI progress in the domain that contextualises the PROMETHEUS RCT.

## Application Areas

| Area | AI Approach | Clinical Status (2023) |
|------|------------|------------------------|
| Standard plane detection | CNNs trained on labelled plane images | Commercially available for some planes |
| Fetal biometry (HC, BPD, FL, AC) | Automated segmentation and measurement | Commercially available; CE/FDA marked products |
| Anomaly detection (structural) | Classification networks; transfer learning | Research phase; limited validation |
| Nuchal translucency measurement | Regression/segmentation | Early clinical tools available |

## Key Claims
- AI has been successfully applied to standard plane detection and biometric measurement — shortening examination time, reducing operator workload, improving diagnostic accuracy in controlled studies
- Combination of AI (acquisition and measurement) + clinician (interpretation) is the dominant practical model
- Remaining challenge: anomaly detection in third trimester is harder than 2D biometry; image quality and fetal position variability limit models

## Limitations Noted
- Most validation studies are single-centre; cross-site generalisation is poorly characterised
- Prospective validation under real-world conditions (varied sonographers, patient demographics) is sparse → PROMETHEUS fills part of this gap
