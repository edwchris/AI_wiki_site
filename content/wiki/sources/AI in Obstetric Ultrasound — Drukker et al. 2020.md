**Citation:** Drukker, L., Noble, J. A., & Papageorghiou, A. T. (2020). Introduction to artificial intelligence in ultrasound imaging in obstetrics and gynecology. *Ultrasound in Obstetrics & Gynecology*, 56(4), 498–505. DOI: 10.1002/uog.22122

**Raw file:** `Drukker, Noble, Papageorghiou - 2020 - Introdu.pdf`

## Summary
Accessible introduction to AI concepts and their applications in obstetric and gynaecological ultrasound, written by researchers from Oxford. Provides conceptual grounding (ML, deep learning, CNNs) and surveys the AI application landscape in fetal imaging. Good entry-point paper for the domain.

## AI Concepts Covered
- Machine learning vs. deep learning distinction
- Convolutional neural networks for image classification
- Supervised vs. unsupervised learning
- Transfer learning

## Application Areas in Obstetric Ultrasound

| Application | Status (2020) |
|-------------|--------------|
| Standard plane detection (anomaly scan) | Active research; early clinical translation |
| Fetal biometry (HC, BPD, FL, AC) | Commercially available tools |
| Fetal weight estimation | AI models comparable to formulae-based |
| Anomaly detection (structural) | Early stage; limited validation |
| Cervical length measurement | Active development |

## Key Observations
- Ultrasound AI faces unique challenges vs. other imaging: real-time acquisition, operator-dependent image quality, high variability in image appearance between operators and sites
- AI plane-detection tools reduce scan variability and support less-experienced operators but cannot replace the interpretive role
- Anticipates the "collaboration vs. conflict" question explored more deeply by [[Sonographer Interaction with AI — Day et al. 2023]]
