## Scope decision: same wiki or separate?

> [!note] This project sits on the boundary. Core ultrasound physics content (wave propagation, transducers, beam formation, artefacts, Doppler) has essentially no overlap with the AI wiki's knowledge base. **Recommendation:** keep this project page here as a connector, but if you develop substantial physics content (definitions, diagrams, worked examples), create a separate `US_Physics` vault for that material.
